About DANIUR

From Turkish Farms To Global Markets With Quality And Care Every Single Harvest

DANIUR is a fresh fruits and vegetables company headquartered in İzmir, Türkiye. With years of experience in the fresh produce industry, we source, prepare and export quality Turkish products to international markets.

Export Process

Our Export Process From Sourcing To International Delivery

Export With Us

01 Sourcing

We work with trusted growers across Türkiye to source fresh produce according to market requirements.

02 Quality Selection

Each batch is inspected and selected to match buyer specifications and quality expectations.

03 Sorting & Grading

Produce is sorted and graded by size, colour, variety and condition before packing.

04 Packaging

Products are packed in export-ready formats such as cartons, wooden boxes, plastic crates and mesh bags.

05 Cold Chain & Logistics

Controlled temperature handling and careful planning keep produce fresh from farm to port.

06 International Delivery

Shipments are coordinated with freight, customs and buyers to reach markets on time.
